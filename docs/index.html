What is a Brier Score?

A Brier score measures probability calibration. It is calculated as (AFG probability − actual outcome)². Lower is better. A perfect score is 0.000. Random guessing scores 0.250. AFG's goal is to score lower than the Kalshi market price on every resolved contract.
