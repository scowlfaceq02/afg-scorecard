## Cross-Asset Themes
- The Iran cluster is now the dominant correlated exposure in the book: two Hormuz strikes plus the US-Iran nuclear deal, all BUY NO, all resting on the same thesis that this conflict resolves far slower than markets price. Three positions, one bet — size as a single unit.
- The Hormuz escalation has a direct transmission channel into the Economics book. Brent above $100 is precisely the inflation shock that would force the Fed's hand, which makes the hike-before-2027 fade and the Hormuz fade partially offsetting rather than independent. If Hormuz resolves faster than AFG expects, the Fed fade improves; if it worsens, the Fed fade weakens.
- Culture favorite premiums (Odyssey at 55%, GTA VI GOTY at 63%) continue to inflate as their events approach rather than deflate — the opposite of the usual pattern, suggesting sustained retail attention rather than informed positioning.
- The July Fed hold BUY YES and the hike-before-2027 BUY NO are not contradictory: they express a single view that the Fed holds at this meeting but the market has overextrapolated the pace of eventual tightening.
