## Cross-Asset Themes
- Tuesday's CPI print moves the December hike, zero cuts in 2026, the recession contract and Citrini together. The end-2027 rate ladder is the one place the print is mispriced mechanically: a higher strike cannot be more likely than a lower one, whatever path the Fed takes.
- The three Heisman positions express one view: Kalshi's board has moved away from the sportsbooks without a game being played. They win together if Carr holds his lead through Notre Dame's last three games and lose together if the books move to Kalshi. Size them as one book.
- The three Bond positions also form one book. If one of Pattinson, Turner or Lowden is cast, that leg loses and the other two win. If Hoult, O'Connor or another actor is cast, all three win.
- The two new Hormuz positions, the January 2028 position held from Monday and the closed Iran deal position share one risk: the Qatar plan. A temporary reopening would lift transits quickly, and a signed nuclear accord would lift both the deal contract and the traffic contracts.
- Turnover after the midterm defeat links the Woodward, Gleason, Hegseth and Scharf contracts. AFG lost the Wiles call by underweighting it, and that loss is why AFG declines to fade Woodward and Gleason without a specific reason.
- The Time Person of the Year prices for Talarico, El-Sayed, Andrews and Turek rest on the same Democratic Senate gains that made the SAVE Act a dead letter.
