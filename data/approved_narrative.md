## Substack Title
Wall Street Hit a Correction on Friday. Is the Fed Market Pricing a Reversal Too Soon?

## Executive Summary
The S&P 500 fell 3.7% on Friday into a correction after Reuters reported an SEC accounting inquiry at Nvidia, whose chief executive Jensen Huang resigned this month. Kalshi's fed funds contract above 4.25% at the end of 2027 dropped from 40 to 33 cents, though Kalshi still gives a January hike 55 to 57 cents. Iranian attacks in the Strait of Hormuz reached their highest level of the war, and Kalshi's Hormuz dates from October 2027 to January 2028 fell 7 to 11 points, to AFG's estimates. Brock Purdy's Sunday night game cut his MVP odds at DraftKings from +400 to +210, and Jeremiah Smith moved to -425 for the Heisman. The White House withdrew its FDA nominee on November 17th, leaving acting commissioner Kyle Diamantas in place.

## Economics
The AI selloff deepened on Friday. Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's chief executive earlier this month, with Colette Kress named interim CEO (Nvidia Form 8-K). The 10-year Treasury yield fell from 5.04% to 4.93%.

## Cross-Asset Themes
- Friday's AI selloff runs through four books. It raises the tail the Citrini position is short and the recession risk that would cost the fed funds position, so those two lose together and should be sized as one view. It also feeds Jensen Huang's price for Google's most-searched person and the AI names in Time's Person of the Year field.
- The fed funds position is a cross-market view inside Kalshi: the January and March meeting contracts price hikes near even odds each, while the year-end 2027 contract prices a fast reversal. A recession call reconciles them; AFG does not see one in the data yet.
- Hormuz links the new July 2028 position, the gas contracts and the Iran deal contracts. An escalation helps the Hormuz position and hurts the gas positions; a settlement does the reverse. A deal before 2028 is the main risk to the July 2028 position.
- Four Hormuz dates closed this cycle because the market fell to AFG's estimates. Closing at fair value locks in no score; only the final published call at settlement counts.
- Purdy's MVP price and the 49ers' Super Bowl price move together; Smith's Heisman and Ohio State's loss both reshaped the college field that sets Georgia's title odds.
- The FDA vacancy ties the Diamantas contract to the Senate's lame-duck calendar: no confirmation, no forced exit.
