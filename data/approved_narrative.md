## Cross-Asset Themes
- Huang's reinstatement and CME's jump in hike odds pull the Citrini and fed funds positions the same way this week: a steadier equity market shrinks the Citrini tail, and a Fed that keeps hiking supports the fed funds position. A recession would hurt both, so they should be sized as one view.
- Inside Kalshi, the January meeting contract prices a hike at 54 to 55 cents against CME's 69%, while the year-end 2027 contract rebounded only to 40 cents. The fed funds position is a bet that Kalshi's curve catches up with futures.
- Purdy's MVP price and the 49ers' Super Bowl price move together, so the two BUY NO positions on San Francisco lose together if the 49ers keep winning.
- Brent near $86 with the strait at about a tenth of normal traffic shows how much of the shock crude has absorbed. That supports the gas positions and leaves the Hormuz curve near AFG's numbers.
- Democrats' control of the Senate in January runs through two contracts: it lowers the odds of a Clarity Act law and is the reason reported for replacing the acting FDA commissioner before then.
- Huang's return raises his own search interest for Google's most-searched person, a contract likely to settle within weeks.
