## Substack Title
Kalshi Says Howard Lutnick Is Out. The Contract Says He Has to Be Gone by December 31st.

## Executive Summary
Reuters reported on Thursday that senior White House officials are discussing whether to push out Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick over the Nvidia export scandal, and a White House official said the president "maintains complete confidence" in him. Kalshi moved his departure this year from 32% to as high as 75% inside a day, but the contract pays only if he actually vacates the job before January 1st, so an exit announced in December and effective in January resolves No. Pete Hegseth's departure price went the other way, from 45% to 15%, after the House shelved an impeachment resolution 211-204 on Tuesday. Kalshi's James Bond board now adds up to about 128% across four actors after Puck reported that Amazon wants Robert Pattinson. Gas fell below $4 a gallon for the first time since July, yet Kalshi still gives 12% to a national average above $4.60 before year-end. CME futures put a half-point Fed hike in December at 12%; Kalshi has 7%.

AFG sets above $4.60 at 4% against 12% (edge -8pp) and above $4.80 at 3% against 11% (edge -8pp): new positions, BUY NO, MEDIUM.

## Cross-Asset Themes
- Hormuz runs through four books at once: the three traffic contracts, the gas-price contracts and the December Fed contracts. A sudden reopening would lower crude, gas and the inflation path together; a strike on Gulf infrastructure would lift the gas contracts and the case for a larger hike. The gas and traffic positions partly offset each other in an escalation and should be sized with that in mind.
- The two gas positions are one bet at two strikes. If the national average passes $4.60 it will very likely pass $4.80 too.
- The December half-point position and the bond selloff are linked: a ten-year yield above 5% is why the futures price a larger hike. A soft November jobs report would undo both.
- The Lutnick and Hegseth positions point in opposite directions on the same question of how much turnover follows the midterm defeat. AFG is short the departure that the market has rushed to price and long the one it has abandoned; both rest on the same 24-case study, which is small, and should be sized as one view.
- The Bond positions form one book. If Pattinson is cast, the Pattinson leg loses and the Turner and Lowden legs win. If Turner or Lowden is cast, that leg loses and the other two win. If Hoult or anyone else is cast, all three win.
- The Heisman positions form one book that wins if the sportsbooks are right and loses together if Kalshi is.
