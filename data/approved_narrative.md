## Cross-Asset Themes
- Hormuz runs through four books: the traffic dates, the gas contracts, the Fed contracts and the Iran deal contracts. An escalation helps the traffic positions and hurts the gas positions; a sudden settlement does the reverse. The two sets partly offset.
- The four Hormuz dates are one view at four strikes, all HIGH. If traffic recovers early, they lose together; size them as a single position.
- The two Clarity Act dates are one view: a lame-duck vehicle before December 11th would settle both Yes at once.
- The fed funds position and the Citrini position lean opposite ways: a deep recession is the path to rates at 4.00% or lower, which would cost the fed funds position and pay the Citrini position.
- The December half-point close and the fed funds position share a reading of the Fed: a gradual pace in December, with more hikes in 2027.
- The two Time positions and the Dancing with the Stars position are attention-driven contracts where viewer and editorial choices are unpublished; all three are SPECULATIVE.
- The Bond settlement shows the cost of fading converging insider reports without a fact of AFG's own; AFG's crowd check flagged the Pattinson call on November 16th, before the announcement.
