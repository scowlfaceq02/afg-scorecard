## Cross-Asset Themes
- Hormuz runs through three books: the traffic dates, the gas contracts and the Iran deal contracts. An escalation helps the traffic positions and hurts the gas positions; a sudden settlement does the reverse. The two sets partly offset.
- The four Hormuz dates are one view at four strikes. If traffic recovers early, they lose together; size them as a single position.
- The AI-led equity selloff touches two positions in opposite directions: it raises the tail the Citrini position is short, and it raises the chance of a recession that would cost the fed funds position.
- Kyle links the hurricane-name and major-hurricane contracts: a fast-strengthening storm would move both, though a major hurricane in late November off the Carolinas would be exceptional.
- The Hassett, Bauer and Lutnick contracts are one question about post-midterm turnover; Hassett's unexplained 14-point fall is the clearest case this week of AFG declining to fade a move it cannot explain.
- Five positions closed this cycle because the market moved to AFG's estimate: Clarity Act (two dates), Mamdani, Frech and GTA VI. Closing at fair value locks in no score; only the final published call at settlement counts.
